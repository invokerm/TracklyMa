# 💰 TracklyMa — Money Tracker

**TracklyMa** is a simple and modern personal finance app designed to help you **track, understand, and manage your money** with ease. 📊💳

📅 **Track Your Spending**
Record your income and expenses and keep a clear overview of where your money goes.

📊 **Daily, Monthly & Yearly Overview**
Review your finances across different time periods and understand your spending habits over time.

💰 **Manage Your Money**
Keep track of your financial activity in one place and stay organized with an easy-to-use interface.

📈 **Understand Your Expenses**
See your spending patterns and get a clearer picture of your financial habits.

### ✨ Features

* 💵 Income & expense tracking
* 📅 Daily financial overview
* 📆 Monthly statistics
* 🗓️ Yearly tracking
* 📊 Spending summaries
* 💰 Financial activity management
* 📈 Expense insights
* 🎨 Clean & modern interface
* 📱 Simple and easy-to-use experience
